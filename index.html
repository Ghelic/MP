SYSTEM

One System. Multiple Missions. F2T2EA

MP SYSTEM is a family of tactical unmanned aerial systems engineered for frontline operators. Designed for rapid deployment and mission adaptability, it delivers critical aerial capabilities directly to the battlefield, empowering individual soldiers and small units with unmatched situational awareness and operational flexibility. The MP SYSTEM portfolio consists of four mission-focused platforms: MP MINI, MP STANDARD, MP SNIPER and MP FORCE UNIT. From intelligence gathering and persistent surveillance to precision mission execution and payload transport, MP SYSTEM provides the versatility required for today's evolving operational environments. Built around a modular architecture and field-proven reliability, every platform is designed to be quick to deploy, easy to operate, simple to maintain, and ready when it matters most. Minimal training, rapid assembly, and streamlined logistics enable operators to focus on the mission—not the equipment. Whether supporting reconnaissance, tactical operations, security missions, or logistics support, MP SYSTEM delivers a powerful force multiplier for modern warfare.

MP
MINI

Compact Reconnaissance UAV Platform

MP MINI is a compact reconnaissance platform designed for pre-operation intelligence and surveillance missions. Featuring a foldable and lightweight airframe, it is easy to carry and rapidly deploy, enabling effective single-operator to be used in field environments. The system is equipped with a high-definition gimbal camera that supports quick detachment and modular assembly for flexible mission adaptation. Powered by a high-performance H7-class processor, the flight controller delivers stable flight performance and real-time data processing. The ground control unit supports both manual remote control and autonomous mission planning, allowing predefined flight routes and reconnaissance tasks to be executed automatically.

MP
SNIPER

Multi-Purpose Heavy-lift Tactical UAV Platform

MP SNIPER is a multi-role intelligent aerial platform designed for a wide range of operational scenarios. The flight control system is powered by a high-performance H7-class processor, enabling high-speed data processing and stable flight control. The airframe features a foldable design, ensuring portability, rapid deployment, and ease of operation in field conditions. The platform is equipped with a high-definition gimbal camera system that supports quick-release and modular replacement, allowing adaptation to diverse mission requirements. The dedicated ground control unit enables real-time remote operation as well as mission planning and waypoint configuration. The UAV can execute pre-programmed autonomous missions, ensuring efficient and reliable task performance. The platform incorporates modular payload interfaces, enabling integration of various mission-specific payloads. Combined with a high-precision stabilized gimbal system, it supports accurate targeting and payload operation. This flexible architecture allows the UAV to be configured for a wide range of operational roles, enhancing overall mission adaptability and effectiveness.

MP FORCE
UNIT

Multi-Purpose Mission Unit

MP FORCE UNIT is a highly integrated mission support unit designed for unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) forces. Built with reinforced composite materials, the interior features customizable partitions and high-density protective foam to ensure secure storage and rapid deployment of mission assets. The UNIT accommodates two multi-rotor UAVs, two stabilized gimbal camera systems, four high-capacity batteries, a ground control unit, and a mission payload carrier module capable of supporting interchangeable operational payloads. The design emphasizes modularity, standardization, and battlefield adaptability, enabling efficient transportation, quick readiness, and reliable support for reconnaissance, surveillance, and mission execution in demanding operational environments.
